StereoSplat+ reconstructs a 3D scene from a single stereo pair. It renders additional stereo views from an initial 3D Gaussian representation, refines those views with a one-step diffusion model, and uses them to update the reconstruction. On KITTI-360, the method improves novel-view rendering and geometry, especially in occluded regions and for viewpoints far from the input cameras.
